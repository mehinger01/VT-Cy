Equation or Inequality?

Equation: two expressions have exactly the same value. Inequality: one side may be greater or less, so more than one value can sometimes work.
x + 3 = 8
x + 3 > 8
2n = 10
2n ≤ 10

Balance Model

Whatever you do to one side of an equation, do to the other side to keep the balance.

=
8
Coach the tutor: What should we remove from both sides first? Why?
x + 4 = 11

3x = 15

One Answer or Many?

x + 3 = 8
Find x. How many answers?
x + 3 > 8
Give four values that work.
x - 2 = 5
Find x.
x - 2 < 5
Give four values that work.
Notice: Equations usually pin down a specific value. Inequalities describe a region or set of possible values.

Translate the Situation

You need exactly 12 blocks.
You need at least 12 blocks.
A game costs exactly $15.
