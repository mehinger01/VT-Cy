Mini Lesson · 12 min

Vertical angles
Opposite angles formed by intersecting lines. They are equal.
Adjacent angles
Angles next to each other that share a side and vertex.
Linear pair
Adjacent angles that form a straight line and total 180°.
Best order
1. Name the relationship.
2. Write the equation.
3. Calculate.

Secondary Literacy Practice · 8 min

Read, Find, Explain

When two lines intersect, they create four angles. Opposite angles are called vertical angles, and they always have equal measures. Angles next to each other form a linear pair when their outside sides make a straight line. A linear pair is supplementary, so its angles total 180 degrees.

Angle Code Challenge

Two lines intersect. One angle measures 126°.
