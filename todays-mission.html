Warm-Up · 5–7 min

Shape Detective

1. Which is not a quadrilateral: triangle, square, rectangle, or trapezoid?
Triangle — it has three sides.

Classify It

2. A shape has four equal sides and four right angles. Name it.
A square.

Line Search

3. Look around you. Find two lines that will never meet. What are they called?
Parallel lines.

Angle Search

4. Find a corner shaped like the corner of a sheet of paper. What kind of angle is it?
A right angle — 90°.

Mini Lesson · 10 min

Square vs. Rectangle
A square is a rectangle with four equal sides.
Parallel
Lines in the same plane that never meet.
Perpendicular
Lines that meet to form right angles.
Angles
Acute < 90° · Right = 90° · Obtuse > 90°.
Symmetry
A line that divides a figure into matching halves.
Measure
Perimeter is around. Area is the space inside.
