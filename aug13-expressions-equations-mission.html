Instruction: Parts of an Expression

Use 3x + 7 and 5a - 2b + 9.

  • Variable: letter standing for a value
  • Coefficient: number multiplying a variable
  • Constant: number without a variable
  • Term: pieces separated by + or −
  • Operation: what the expression tells us to do
Label every part of 4m + 9
How many terms in 2x + 3y - 6?
Coefficient of p in 7p - 5
Constants in 3a + 8 - 2b

Guided Practice: Evaluate

x=4: 3x+2
n=6: 2n-5
a=3,b=4: 2a+b
m=5: 4(m+1)

Bridge: Expression or Equation?

An expression names a quantity. An equation says two quantities are equal.

5x+3
5x+3=18
2n-7
2n-7=9

One-Step Equation Method

1. Identify what is happening to the variable. 2. Undo it using the inverse operation. 3. Do the same to both sides. 4. Check.

x+6=14
3x=21
n-8=5
m/4=6

Exit Ticket

Name the coefficient, variable, and constant in 6x+11.
Evaluate 2x+5 when x=7.
Solve 4x=28 and show the step.
Explain the difference between an expression and equation.
