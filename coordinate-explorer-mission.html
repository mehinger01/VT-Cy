Reading Connection · 8 min

How Ordered Pairs Work

An ordered pair tells the location of a point on a coordinate plane. The first number shows how far to move horizontally along the x-axis. The second number shows how far to move vertically along the y-axis. Following the coordinates in the correct order helps us plot points accurately.
